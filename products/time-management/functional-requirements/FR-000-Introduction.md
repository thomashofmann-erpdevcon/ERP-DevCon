# Functional Requirements

## Purpose

This document introduces the Functional Requirements for the ERP DevCon Time Management product.

The Functional Requirements describe **what** the product shall accomplish from a business perspective.

They intentionally avoid implementation details and technology-specific considerations.

The Functional Requirements constitute the contractual business specification for the product and provide the basis for all subsequent engineering activities.

---

# Product Vision

The ERP DevCon Time Management product provides a comprehensive platform for managing working time and all related business processes.

The product is intended to support organizations of different sizes and industries while remaining highly configurable and extensible.

The system shall enable organizations to

- record business-relevant time information,
- manage employee attendance,
- administer absences and leave,
- support workforce planning,
- provide legally compliant documentation,
- deliver reliable business information,
- integrate with surrounding business systems.

The product shall support organizations without prescribing their internal processes.

---

# Engineering Principles

The Time Management product follows the engineering principles defined by the ERP DevCon Company Guide.

In particular

- Business before Technology
- Information before Automation
- Business Objects and Business Transactions
- Ledger-Based Architecture
- Single Source of Truth
- Historical Correctness
- Traceability throughout the Engineering Lifecycle

shall govern all business and engineering decisions.

---

# Scope of the Functional Requirements

The Functional Requirements define

- business objectives,
- business terminology,
- business domain,
- business objects,
- business transactions,
- business rules,
- business processes,
- required business behaviour,
- reporting requirements,
- integration requirements,
- non-functional requirements.

Implementation details belong to the Solution Design and Technical Design documentation.

# Document Structure

The Functional Requirements are divided into independent engineering documents.

Each document describes one specific aspect of the business domain.

This structure improves

- maintainability,
- traceability,
- version control,
- reviewability,
- reuse.

---

# Traceability

Every functional requirement shall remain traceable to

- business objectives,
- business rules,
- business objects,
- business transactions,
- solution design,
- technical design,
- implementation,
- testing.

Traceability shall be preserved throughout the complete engineering lifecycle.
