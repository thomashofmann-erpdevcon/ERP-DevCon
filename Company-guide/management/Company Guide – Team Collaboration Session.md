# Company Guide – Team Collaboration Session

## Purpose

The Team Collaboration Session establishes a predictable and efficient collaboration model that supports focused work while providing regular opportunities for communication, knowledge sharing and decision making.

It is a recurring collaboration window rather than a traditional meeting.

---

# Objectives

The Team Collaboration Session provides an opportunity to:

- answer questions,
- remove blockers,
- discuss technical and architectural topics,
- review ideas and proposed solutions,
- share experience and lessons learned,
- exchange knowledge across the team,
- make decisions when required.

The session is not limited to solving problems. It is equally intended to strengthen the collective knowledge of the team.

---

# Principles

Focused work is essential for every team member.

Interruptions should be minimised whenever possible.

Collaboration should be predictable, prepared and driven by actual demand rather than by a fixed meeting agenda.

The collaboration window belongs to the entire team. Every team member contributes experience, asks questions, shares knowledge and participates in discussions.

Management responsibility is exercised only when organisational or business decisions require it. In all other situations, discussions are collaborative and every team member participates as an equal contributor.

---

# Demand-Driven Collaboration

The Team Collaboration Session reserves a recurring time slot for collaboration.

Whether the session actually takes place depends entirely on the topics submitted by the team.

If no topics have been submitted before the scheduled collaboration window, no meeting takes place.

No cancellation is required.

The absence of topics simply indicates that no collaborative discussion is currently needed.

---

# Topic Ownership

Every discussion topic has an owner.

The topic owner is responsible for preparing the discussion and providing sufficient information for the team.

Each topic should contain:

- a concise title,
- a short description,
- the expected outcome,
- relevant background information,
- links or attachments where appropriate,
- the Required Participants.

---

# Required Participants

The topic owner identifies the team members whose contribution is required to resolve the topic or make a decision.

Required Participants are explicitly named when the topic is published.

This allows every team member to understand whether their participation is expected without unnecessary coordination.

---

# Optional Participation

Team members are encouraged to participate even when they are not Required Participants if they:

- expect to learn from the discussion,
- wish to broaden their technical or business knowledge,
- believe they can contribute useful experience,
- may work on similar topics in the future.

Participation is therefore based on both contribution and learning.

---

# Topic Publication

Topics should be published in the Microsoft Teams chat assigned to the Team Collaboration Session as soon as they arise.

Publishing topics throughout the day allows every team member to:

- review the agenda,
- prepare contributions,
- decide whether participation is valuable,
- organise focused work accordingly.

The published topics form the agenda for the collaboration session.

---

# Knowledge Sharing

Team members are encouraged to share knowledge even when no assistance is required.

Examples include:

* solutions to recently solved problems,
* useful implementation techniques,
* architectural decisions and their rationale,
* process improvements,
* lessons learned,
* discoveries that may benefit other team members.

Knowledge sharing is considered an investment in the capability of the entire team.

Topics intended primarily for knowledge sharing should be published in advance like any other discussion topic, allowing interested team members to decide whether they wish to participate.

---

# Meeting Documentation

The primary purpose of the Team Collaboration Session is collaboration.

Participants should actively contribute to the discussion and should not be distracted by producing detailed meeting notes.

Whenever appropriate, Microsoft Teams transcription should be enabled.

The use of transcription is encouraged because it allows participants to focus entirely on the discussion while preserving the content of the session.

Video recording should be used selectively when visual information is important, for example during:

* presentations,
* demonstrations,
* training sessions,
* product walkthroughs.

For most technical discussions, architecture reviews and question-and-answer sessions, a transcription is sufficient.

---

## Meeting Summary

At the beginning of every recorded or transcribed session, one participant shall be designated as the Documentation Owner.

The Documentation Owner is responsible for preparing the final meeting summary.

The summary should be based on the meeting transcription and may be supported by Artificial Intelligence tools.

AI is encouraged to assist with:

* improving the transcription,
* producing an initial summary,
* identifying decisions,
* identifying action items,
* improving readability and structure.

The Documentation Owner remains responsible for verifying the correctness and completeness of the final summary.

---

## Knowledge Availability

Meeting summaries should be made available to the team after the session.

This enables team members who could not participate to benefit from the discussion and reduces the need to repeat knowledge-sharing sessions.

The combination of focused collaboration during the meeting and structured documentation afterwards supports efficient knowledge transfer while protecting the attention of active participants.

---

# Benefits

The Team Collaboration Session:

* protects focused working time,
* reduces unnecessary interruptions,
* enables self-organised participation,
* encourages knowledge sharing,
* increases transparency,
* reduces dependency on individual experts,
* supports continuous professional development,
* provides predictable access to collective experience,
* creates reusable knowledge through structured meeting summaries.

---

# Summary

The Team Collaboration Session is a recurring collaboration window owned by the team.

Its agenda is created by the team through published topics.

The topic owner prepares each discussion, identifies the Required Participants and defines the expected outcome.

Team members participate either because their contribution is required or because they wish to learn and share experience.

Discussions focus on collaboration rather than documentation. Knowledge is preserved through transcriptions, AI-assisted meeting summaries and, where appropriate, video recordings.

The result is an efficient, demand-driven collaboration model that supports focused work, continuous learning and sustainable knowledge sharing across the team.
