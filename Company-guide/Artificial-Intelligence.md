# Artificial Intelligence

## Purpose

This document defines the principles governing the responsible and effective use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) within ERP DevCon.

Artificial Intelligence is regarded as an engineering assistant that supports human expertise.

The responsibility for engineering decisions always remains with qualified engineers.

---

# Scope

These principles apply to

- Business Analysis
- Documentation
- Software Architecture
- Software Development
- Testing
- Research
- Knowledge Management

and all future engineering activities where AI systems are used.

---

# CG-012.1 AI is an Engineering Assistant

Artificial Intelligence supports engineering work.

AI does not replace engineering responsibility.

Business, architectural and technical decisions shall always be reviewed by qualified engineers.

---

# CG-012.2 Human Responsibility

Final responsibility for

- business requirements,
- architecture,
- implementation,
- documentation,
- software quality

remains with ERP DevCon.

AI-generated content shall always be reviewed before adoption.

---

# CG-012.3 Transparency

The origin of engineering knowledge shall remain understandable.

Whenever AI significantly contributes to engineering artefacts, contributors should ensure that the resulting documentation remains understandable without requiring access to the AI conversation.

Engineering decisions shall be documented explicitly.

---

# CG-012.4 Verification

AI-generated content shall be verified before it becomes part of the Engineering Repository.

Verification includes

- business correctness,
- engineering consistency,
- terminology,
- architectural compliance,
- completeness.

AI output shall never be accepted solely because it appears plausible.

---

# CG-012.5 Business before AI

Artificial Intelligence shall never redefine business requirements.

Business Analysis remains a human engineering activity.

AI may assist by

- identifying inconsistencies,
- improving wording,
- proposing alternatives,
- supporting analysis.

Business intent shall always originate from the responsible engineers.

---

# CG-012.6 Knowledge Preservation

Important engineering knowledge shall be transferred into the Engineering Repository.

Knowledge shall not remain exclusively inside AI conversations.

The Engineering Repository is the authoritative company knowledge base.

---

# CG-012.7 Documentation Quality

AI may assist in improving

- readability,
- consistency,
- terminology,
- document structure,
- completeness.

Engineering quality shall always have priority over document quantity.

---

# CG-012.8 Software Development

AI may support software development by

- explaining existing code,
- reviewing implementations,
- generating prototypes,
- identifying potential defects,
- proposing improvements.

Generated software shall undergo the same engineering review as manually written software.

---

# CG-012.9 Architecture

Architectural decisions shall never be delegated to AI.

AI may assist by

- analysing alternatives,
- identifying consequences,
- documenting decisions,
- improving consistency.

Architecture Decision Records shall remain the authoritative record of engineering decisions.

---

# CG-012.10 Confidentiality

The use of AI shall comply with company policies regarding

- confidentiality,
- customer information,
- intellectual property,
- legal obligations,
- contractual restrictions.

Confidential information shall only be processed using approved AI services.

---

# CG-012.11 Continuous Learning

Engineering teams are encouraged to continuously improve their effective use of AI.

Experience gained through practical usage should be documented and incorporated into company standards where appropriate.

---

# CG-012.12 Engineering Mindset

AI shall encourage engineering thinking rather than replace it.

The objective of AI usage is

- better understanding,
- higher quality,
- improved consistency,
- faster documentation,
- more informed engineering decisions.

AI shall never become a substitute for engineering judgement.
