# Company Guide

## Purpose

The Company Guide defines the engineering principles, development standards and organizational rules that apply throughout ERP DevCon.

It serves as the authoritative reference for company-wide engineering practices and establishes a common understanding for business analysis, software architecture, software development, documentation and quality management.

The Company Guide intentionally remains independent from individual software products.

Product-specific requirements shall be documented within the corresponding product documentation.

---

# Objectives

The objectives of the Company Guide are to

- establish common engineering principles,
- define consistent terminology,
- document company-wide standards,
- improve maintainability,
- support long-term product development,
- preserve engineering knowledge,
- ensure consistent documentation.

---

# Scope

The Company Guide applies to

- all software products,
- reusable software libraries,
- internal development tools,
- engineering documentation,
- software architecture,
- software development,
- technical documentation,
- quality assurance.

Business requirements that apply exclusively to an individual product are outside the scope of this guide.

---

# Structure

The Company Guide is organized into independent chapters.

Each chapter covers a single engineering domain.

Typical chapters include

- Company Philosophy
- Documentation Standards
- Architecture Principles
- Software Engineering
- Coding Standards
- Quality Management
- Knowledge Management
- Artificial Intelligence
- Project Management
- Glossary

Additional chapters may be added whenever required.

---

# Relationship to Other Documents

The Company Guide defines company-wide engineering principles.

It is complemented by

- Architecture Decision Records (ADR),
- Technical Standards,
- Product Documentation,
- Functional Requirements,
- Solution Designs,
- Technical Designs.

The Company Guide references these documents where appropriate but does not duplicate their contents.

---

# Guiding Principles

The following principles govern the development and maintenance of this guide.

## Business before Technology

Business requirements define software.

Technology implements business requirements.

---

## Single Source of Truth

Every engineering concept shall have one authoritative definition.

Duplicate definitions shall be avoided.

---

## Separation of Concerns

Company-wide standards shall remain independent from product-specific documentation.

---

## Long-Term Maintainability

Engineering documentation shall be written with the expectation that it remains useful throughout the lifetime of the software products it supports.

---

## Continuous Improvement

The Company Guide is considered a living document.

Improvements shall be introduced incrementally while preserving consistency and traceability.

---

# Intended Audience

The Company Guide is intended for

- Product Owners
- Business Analysts
- Software Architects
- Software Developers
- Technical Writers
- Test Engineers
- Project Managers

Other stakeholders may use the guide as a reference whenever appropriate.

---

# Document Maintenance

The Company Guide is maintained within the ERP DevCon Engineering Repository.

Changes shall follow the defined contribution and review process.

Only approved content shall be merged into the main branch.
