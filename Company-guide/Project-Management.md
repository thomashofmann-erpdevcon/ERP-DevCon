# Project Management

## Purpose

This document defines the project management principles applied by ERP DevCon.

Project Management coordinates engineering activities throughout the complete product lifecycle while ensuring that business objectives remain the primary driver of all project decisions.

Project Management is regarded as an enabling discipline rather than an administrative function.

---

# Scope

These principles apply to

- product development,
- product maintenance,
- internal projects,
- research projects,
- customer projects.

---

# CG-013.1 Business Value

Projects shall be driven by business value.

Planning, prioritization and resource allocation shall maximize the long-term value delivered to customers and the organization.

Administrative activities shall support engineering work rather than become objectives of their own.

---

# CG-013.2 Engineering before Administration

Engineering activities create value.

Administrative activities support engineering.

Project management shall therefore minimize unnecessary administrative effort.

Documentation shall only be produced where it provides measurable engineering value.

---

# CG-013.3 Incremental Delivery

Projects should progress through small, reviewable increments.

Each increment should

- produce measurable progress,
- reduce uncertainty,
- improve the product,
- increase engineering knowledge.

Long implementation phases without review should be avoided.

---

# CG-013.4 Traceable Decisions

Important project decisions shall be documented.

Project decisions should remain understandable long after their implementation.

Architectural decisions belong in Architecture Decision Records.

Product decisions belong in the corresponding Decision Log.

---

# CG-013.5 Requirements Drive Planning

Project planning shall be based on approved requirements.

Schedules shall reflect

- business priorities,
- engineering dependencies,
- implementation complexity,
- available resources.

Schedules shall not redefine business priorities.

---

# CG-013.6 Risk Management

Risks shall be identified as early as reasonably possible.

Risk management should focus on

- business risks,
- technical risks,
- architectural risks,
- schedule risks,
- operational risks.

Risk mitigation shall be documented whenever appropriate.

---

# CG-013.7 Transparency

Project status shall remain transparent.

Stakeholders should always be able to understand

- current scope,
- completed work,
- remaining work,
- open decisions,
- identified risks.

Transparency supports informed business decisions.

---

# CG-013.8 Change Management

Change is regarded as a normal aspect of software development.

Requested changes shall be

- analysed,
- documented,
- evaluated,
- prioritized.

Deferred changes shall remain visible within the Change Request Backlog.

---

# CG-013.9 Reviews

Engineering reviews shall occur regularly.

Reviews verify

- business correctness,
- engineering quality,
- architectural consistency,
- documentation completeness,
- project progress.

Reviews shall improve the product rather than merely report status.

---

# CG-013.10 Continuous Improvement

Project execution shall continuously improve.

Lessons learned from completed work should influence

- planning,
- engineering standards,
- documentation,
- estimation,
- collaboration.

Continuous improvement is considered part of every project.
